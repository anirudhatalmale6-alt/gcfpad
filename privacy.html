Privacy Policy

What we collect when you contact us, and what we do with it.

Review before publishingThis is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Have it checked against the law of the country GCFPAD is registered in before the site goes live, and tell me the registered entity name and address so they can be named here.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry we receive the name, organization, email address, country, area of interest and message you type into the form. We do not collect anything else through this website.

Why we hold it

To answer your inquiry and, where it leads to a partnership, to carry out that work. We do not sell it and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

How long

Inquiries are kept for as long as the relationship is active, and then deleted.

Cookies

This website sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. The only external request it makes is for the typefaces.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the contact form and say so.
