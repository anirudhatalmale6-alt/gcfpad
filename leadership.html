Structure

How GCFPAD is organized

General Assembly

The members of the confederation, meeting to set direction and approve the programme of work.

Executive Board

Responsible for strategy, oversight and the appointment of the executive leadership.

Secretariat

Day-to-day coordination, partnerships and programme delivery.

Advisory Council

Independent expertise across the areas GCFPAD works in.

Audit & Integrity

Financial controls, safeguarding and conflict of interest.

Regional Chapters

Representation close to where the work is carried out.

Confirm before publishingThese six bodies are the conventional structure for a confederation of this kind. Confirm or correct them against your statutes before this page goes live - I would rather you change six headings than have a partner read something that is not how you are actually governed.
